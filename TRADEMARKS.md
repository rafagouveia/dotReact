You may:

- accurately state that an application is "built with DotReact" or "compatible
  with DotReact";
- link to the official repository or documentation;
- use the word DotReact to describe, teach, review or contribute to the project;
- preserve the official attribution included in generated projects and package
  notices.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, certification or endorsement by Black
Red Solutions.

## Forks and redistributed framework builds

You may modify, redistribute and sell the open-source framework under MPL 2.0.
If a build or fork is not an official Black Red Solutions release, it must use a
distinct product name and visual identity. Do not publish it as `DotReact`, use
the official logo as its primary brand, reuse official package or publisher
identifiers, or describe it as an official release.

You may truthfully state that a fork is "based on DotReact" and must preserve
copyright and license notices required by MPL 2.0.

## Applications built with DotReact

Commercial and proprietary applications may use DotReact. They do not need to
use the DotReact name or logo in their interface. When an application
redistributes DotReact packages or covered source files, preserve the generated
`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` attribution or provide an equivalent source and license
notice that complies with MPL 2.0.

Contact the maintainers through the repository for uses not covered here. This
policy describes permitted brand use and does not replace legal advice.
